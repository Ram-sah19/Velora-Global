# Explore Internships Skill

Use this skill to answer questions about Velora Global internships: which domains are offered, how long a track runs, what mentorship costs, and how a student applies.

## What an internship is
Project-driven work experience. The intern builds deliverables in the domain they pick and is reviewed by a mentor; each submission is scored against published criteria and returned with written remarks. Graduates receive a certificate with a unique verification ID.

## Domains offered
Frontend Development, Backend Development, Full Stack Development, Mobile App Development, Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Data Science, Cybersecurity, UI/UX Design, Cloud & DevOps, Software Testing, JavaScript & Modern ES6+, Java (Core, Spring Boot & Microservices), Python (Scripting & Automation).

## Duration
The applicant chooses the length at the time of applying: 2 Weeks, 1 Month, 2 Months, 3 Months, 6 Months.

## Mentorship levels
The fee is a one-time charge set by the level, not by the duration. Enrolment is confirmed once the fee is received; the amount and payment instructions are sent after you apply.

- **Basic** NPR 500 — Resume review, introductory career guidance, and group mentorship.
- **Standard** NPR 999 — Resume, LinkedIn, GitHub, and a defined one-to-one mentorship session.
- **Advanced** NPR 1,999 — More extensive one-to-one mentorship, portfolio feedback, mock interview practice, and a personalized career action plan.

## How to apply
Applications are submitted through the internship application form linked from `https://velora-global.online/internships`. A registered student account can also track submitted applications and assigned tasks through the student workspace.

## Related endpoints
- Program list: `GET https://velora-global.online/api/programs?domain=&search=`
- Certificate check: `GET https://velora-global.online/api/certificates/verify/:certificateId`
- Full spec: `https://velora-global.online/openapi.json`
